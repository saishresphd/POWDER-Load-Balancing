Paper: NaU = active (traffic) UEs, PaU = W/active-UE;
       NUi = idle UEs,           PUi = W/idle-UE

  Estimated parameters from collected data:
    Pbase = 15.158 W  (measured ue=0, no UEs)
    PaU   = 0.2748 W/UE  (slope ue=0→9, near-zero traffic)
    PUi   = 0.2992 W/UE  (mean marginal W/active-UE, ue=14-34)

  Paper reference values (their low-power hardware):
    Pbase = 4.04 W  |  Adding 1 idle UE → 4.08 W  →  PaU≈0.04 W/UE
  Our hardware (d430 server) operates at higher absolute power,
